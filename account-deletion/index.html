Account Deletion Request

Your data, your choice. Here's everything you need to know about deleting your Rebootr® account.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the app at any time. No need to contact support.

  1. Open the Rebootr® app on your device
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted

Account deletion is processed immediately upon confirmation. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account profile and credentials
  • All progress and milestone records
  • Daily wins and tracking history
  • Any personal data stored in our database

Data categories removed:

Account info Progress data Daily wins Tracking history User preferences

Processing Time & Retention

Deletion is triggered immediately once you confirm within the app. Your data is removed from our active systems at that time.

Certain anonymized or aggregated data (not linked to your identity) may be retained for analytical purposes as permitted by applicable law.

Need Help?

If you're unable to delete your account from within the app or have any questions about your data, contact our support team:

Email us at support@rebootr.app — we'll respond within 48 hours and process your request promptly.
